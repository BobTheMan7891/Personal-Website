FAQs

How long does a project take?

Turnaround depend on your chosen package and additional services: Basic sites will typically be 1-2 weeks, Standard 2-3 weeks, and premium 3-5 weeks. These are estimates and I'll confirm a more exact timeline once we've discussed your project.

How do I place an order?

To get started, fill out the contact form (link below) with the service(s) you're interested in. I'll get back to you directly so we can tailor a package that fits what you need and discuss any other relevant details. I will repsond as soon as I can, usually within one or two business days.

Contact form

How does the payment work?

Once your package has been finalised, I'll send a secure Stripe payment link for a 50% deposit to begin the work. The remaining 50% will be due when you are happy with the result or all revisions have been exhausted.

What's not included in the price?

Domain registration and any paid stock images or fonts you choose aren't included. I'll help you find good free alternatives if you'd prefer to keep costs down.

Can I cancel hosting later?

Yes, hosting is billed on the first of every month with no lock-in contract. I'll help move your site elsewhere if you ever want to leave.
